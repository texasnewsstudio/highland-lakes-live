LLANO COUNTY WEIGHS THE FUTURE OF COUNTYWIDE VOTING

Commissioners meet Monday to discuss continued use of the countywide polling-place program.

Llano County commissioners have a special meeting scheduled for 9 a.m. Monday, November 16 to discuss and potentially act on continued participation in Texas's countywide polling-place program. The program can affect where voters cast ballots. The agenda signals a decision point, not a confirmed change to election procedures.

Why this matters locally

The Highland Lakes region is changing, and decisions about infrastructure, schools, public services and community life affect residents across city boundaries. Highland Lakes Live will distinguish scheduled actions and reported developments from final outcomes.
