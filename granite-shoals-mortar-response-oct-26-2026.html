Granite Shoals Police Respond to Old Practice Mortar Found in Home

Burnet County · October 27, 2026

A discovery inside a Granite Shoals home brought a precautionary police response over the weekend after a resident reported what appeared to be an unexploded military item.

Authorities established a temporary safety perimeter on Saturday, October 24, while specialists assessed the object. Police later identified it as an old military practice or training mortar and determined it did not present an explosive threat.

The incident ended without a reported explosion or injury. It is a reminder that unfamiliar military-looking objects should be left undisturbed and reported to authorities rather than handled.
