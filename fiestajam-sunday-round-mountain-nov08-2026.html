FIESTAJAM SUNDAY BRINGS TEXAS MUSIC TO ROUND MOUNTAIN

The three-day songwriter celebration reaches its Sunday program with Texas performers on the bill at Round Mountain Cider Mill.

November 8, 2026

FiestaJAM reaches its Sunday finale with a lineup rooted in Texas music, giving Highland Lakes residents one more chance to catch the three-day celebration of songs and the people who write them.

The 2026 event moved to Round Mountain Cider Mill, a newer venue with both indoor gathering areas and an outdoor stage. Festival coverage identifies Anthony Garcia, John Arthur Martinez and Josh Baca on the Sunday bill at 3 p.m., followed by Rubén Ramos and The Mexican Revolution at 4:30 p.m.

The setting is part of the story. Regional festivals can draw audiences across county lines, introducing visitors to businesses and communities that they might otherwise pass on the highway. A music event in Round Mountain also broadens the region’s entertainment map beyond the most familiar Marble Falls venues.

Live-event details can change, especially when weather affects outdoor performances. Anyone planning to attend should confirm ticket availability, start times and venue rules directly with FiestaJAM before leaving home.

The festival’s larger purpose is to celebrate songwriting rather than simply fill a concert schedule. That makes the performers’ stories and musical traditions as important as the headline acts.
