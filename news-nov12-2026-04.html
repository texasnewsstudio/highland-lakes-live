MARBLE FALLS PREPARES FOR FRIDAY'S CHRISTMAS TREE LIGHTING

Harmony Park is scheduled to host the lighting of the city's large tree at around 6 p.m. November 13.

The city of Marble Falls plans to light its large Christmas tree at Harmony Park on Friday, November 13, at dusk, approximately 6 p.m. Local reporting describes refreshments, family-friendly activities and a screening of The Grinch after the lighting. Visitors should check for schedule changes before attending.
