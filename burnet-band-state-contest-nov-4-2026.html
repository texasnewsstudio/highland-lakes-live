Burnet Band Advances to State Marching Contest

November 4, 2026 · Highland Lakes Live

Burnet High School’s Esprit de Corps marching band is headed to the UIL Class 4A State Marching Contest after earning a state berth through its area competition performances.

Local reporting published November 3 says the band delivered two strong area performances on its way to qualifying. The achievement brings attention to the work of Burnet students, directors and supporters as marching season continues.

Families interested in attending should consult Burnet school and UIL announcements for the official contest schedule, venue and ticket details before traveling.

Source: DailyTrib, November 3 reporting
