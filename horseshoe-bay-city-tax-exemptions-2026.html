Horseshoe Bay Homeowners: Know the City Tax Rate and Exemptions

The city lists a rate of $0.26775 per $100 and explains its homestead exemption for eligible primary residences.

The City of Horseshoe Bay lists its current property-tax rate as $0.26775 per $100 of taxable value. The city says eligible full-time residents may qualify for a 20% city homestead exemption. The city portion is only one component of a complete tax bill, which can include county, school and special-district taxes.

For property-specific questions, check official documents and your applicable taxing or utility authorities. This report summarizes the cited public information and does not assert new local project approvals.
