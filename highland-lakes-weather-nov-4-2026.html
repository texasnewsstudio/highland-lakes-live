Highland Lakes Weather November 4: Sunshine and Comfortable Temperatures

November 4, 2026 · Highland Lakes Live

Wednesday, November 4, brings a comfortable break from recent unsettled weather across the Highland Lakes and much of the Texas Hill Country. The National Weather Service Austin/San Antonio forecast discussion issued early Wednesday calls for dry air, sunshine and daytime highs generally in the 70s.

Morning temperatures across parts of the Hill Country started in the 40s, with a mild afternoon expected. Conditions can vary between Marble Falls, Burnet, Kingsland, Llano and lakeside communities.

The next weather change could arrive with another frontal passage Thursday or Friday. A second front is possible over the weekend, but the timing and any rain chances remain uncertain. Check current National Weather Service forecasts and radar before making outdoor plans.

This forecast reflects National Weather Service guidance available early Wednesday, November 4, 2026, and is not a live forecast feed.

Source: National Weather Service Austin/San Antonio, November 4 forecast discussion
