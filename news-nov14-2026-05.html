THE POTATO SHACK RELOCATES IN KINGSLAND

November 14, 2026 · Regional news roundup

A familiar local food business opens at a different address.

The Potato Shack has opened at 106 Cottonwood Drive, Suite C, in Kingsland, according to DailyTrib's November 12 business report. The relocation is one of the small-business developments shaping the Highland Lakes retail and dining landscape. Check directly with the business for current operating hours.
