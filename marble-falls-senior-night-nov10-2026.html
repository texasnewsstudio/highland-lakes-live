MARBLE FALLS HONORS CLASS OF 2027 AT SENIOR NIGHT

The Mustangs recognized seniors before their November 6 season finale.

Marble Falls High School celebrated members of the Class of 2027 during senior-night activities before the Mustangs’ final football game of the season on Friday, November 6.

The recognition highlighted students and families whose contributions extend beyond the final score. The game against Stephenville marked the end of the Mustangs’ 2026 football campaign.

As the football season closes, attention turns toward winter athletics and the remaining school calendar. Local reporting published November 9 included a photo gallery of the senior-night recognition.

Reporting basis: November 9 local reporting. Highland Lakes Live update: November 10, 2026.
