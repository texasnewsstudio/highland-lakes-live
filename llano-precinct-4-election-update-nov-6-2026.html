Llano County Precinct 4 Incumbent Retains Commission Seat

November 6, 2026 · Highland Lakes Live News Desk

Jerry Don Moss won another term representing Precinct 4, according to local election coverage.

Jerry Don Moss retained his seat on the Llano County Commissioners Court in the November 3 election, according to local reporting published November 4. County commissioners make decisions about roads, budgets and other services that affect residents.
