Final Week of Early Voting Underway in Burnet and Llano Counties

Regional News · October 27, 2026

Early voting for the November 3 election is in its final week, with the advance voting period running through Friday, October 30.

Voters in Burnet and Llano counties have federal and state contests on the ballot, along with local races depending on their precinct. Llano County voters also have a Precinct 4 commissioner contest.

Before leaving home, voters should confirm their polling location, voting hours and eligibility details with their county elections office.
