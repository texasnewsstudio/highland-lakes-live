HIGHLAND LAKES 'LOCALS LOVE US' VOTING LOOKS AHEAD TO 2027

The annual community favorites contest is accepting attention from area readers and businesses.

HIGHLAND LAKES LIVE · Original report or event listing: November 4, 2026

The Picayune and KBEY's annual Locals Love Us contest is back for its 2027 edition, giving Highland Lakes residents a chance to recognize favorite local businesses and community destinations. The contest was announced in early November. Readers should consult the organizers for voting rules and deadlines.
