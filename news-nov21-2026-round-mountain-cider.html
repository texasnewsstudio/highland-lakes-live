COUNTRY MUSIC MEETS CIDER! KEVIN FOWLER HEADLINES SATURDAY NIGHT

Stars, Strings and Cider returns to Round Mountain for its second year.

Round Mountain Cider Mill is scheduled to host the second annual Stars, Strings and Cider on Saturday, November 21, with Kevin Fowler featured on the bill. The event listing gives a 7 to 10 p.m. window at 222 Lee Lane.

For regional music fans, the concert offers a weekend destination beyond the larger towns. Check directly with the venue for tickets, parking and any schedule changes.
