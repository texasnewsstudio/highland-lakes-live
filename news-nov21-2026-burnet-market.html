LAST MARKET SATURDAY! BURNET'S FALL CRAFTS SEASON WRAPS UP

Downtown Burnet hosts a final scheduled Saturday farmers and crafts market.

Burnet's Farmers and Crafts Market is scheduled for 9 a.m. to 1 p.m. on Saturday, November 21. The event calendar identifies this date as the last Saturday in its current market run.

The market offers a local shopping option ahead of Thanksgiving, with food and handmade goods among the advertised attractions. Shoppers should verify vendor details and any weather-related changes with the event organizer.
