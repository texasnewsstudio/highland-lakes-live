LCRA REPORTS NO FLOODGATE OPERATIONS EXPECTED AT HIGHLAND LAKES DAMS

The November 18 morning update shows no gate operations expected at major regional dams.

The Lower Colorado River Authority's flood operations page showed no floodgate operations expected at Buchanan, Wirtz, Starcke, Mansfield and other listed dams in its November 18, 6:40 a.m. update. The status is a snapshot rather than a guarantee that conditions will remain unchanged.

Recent rainfall and runoff have made river conditions an important issue across the Highland Lakes. LCRA's broader flood page notes that the system can continue moving storm runoff through hydroelectric generation even when floodgates are not open. That distinction matters for people who live, work or recreate near the water.

LCRA also notes that Inks Dam does not have floodgates and is designed for floodwater to pass over its spillway. Residents should consult LCRA's current Hydromet and flood operations updates for changes before making decisions involving lake access or downstream conditions.
