FIVE MARBLE FALLS SENIORS TAKE THEIR GAME TO COLLEGE

Five student-athletes signed to compete at the collegiate level.

A milestone at Marble Falls High School put five seniors in the spotlight: Madison Cuplin, Sidney Solomon, Amaris Mitchell-Ochoa, Jocelyn Suarez and Addie Moomaw signed to continue their sports careers in college, according to November 13 reporting. Their signings highlight the commitment behind student athletics and the opportunities available beyond high school.
