Outdoor Activities Around Burnet County

How to prepare for parks, trails and day trips.

Burnet County and the surrounding Highland Lakes region offer a mix of parks, scenic drives and water-based recreation. Conditions, access rules and operating hours differ by location.

Before visiting, check the destination’s official website for fees, reservations and closures. Carry water, use sun protection and avoid exposed areas during thunderstorms.

During dry weather, verify local burn restrictions before planning any activity involving fire. For a safer outing, pair a destination plan with current weather and road information.
