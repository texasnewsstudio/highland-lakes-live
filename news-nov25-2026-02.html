RAINBOW TROUT ARE COMING! ANGLERS GET A HOLIDAY TREAT

Highland Lakes Live News Desk | November 25, 2026

Texas Parks and Wildlife is releasing thousands of rainbow trout in the Highland Lakes region, local reporting said November 24. Anglers should consult the agency’s stocking schedule and fishing regulations before heading out.
