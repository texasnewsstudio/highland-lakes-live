GRANITE SHOALS REVISITS ITS 2010 CITY PLAN

Council will consider the comprehensive plan alongside paving and flood-warning agreements.

Granite Shoals City Council is scheduled to discuss its 2010 comprehensive plan on November 17, along with a proposed paving-assistance agreement with Burnet County and an agreement involving early flood-warning equipment. These topics touch three major concerns for a growing lake community: long-range land use, streets and emergency preparedness.

Why this matters locally

The Highland Lakes region is changing, and decisions about infrastructure, schools, public services and community life affect residents across city boundaries. Highland Lakes Live will distinguish scheduled actions and reported developments from final outcomes.
