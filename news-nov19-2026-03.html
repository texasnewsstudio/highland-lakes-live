WATER RULE SHAKE-UP? LCRA PROPOSAL COULD REACH LOCAL LAWNS

Potential changes to firm water contracts could affect outdoor watering rules.

The Lower Colorado River Authority is considering changes to its firm water contract rules, including provisions that could affect landscape watering, according to November 18 local reporting.

The proposal is under consideration rather than a final rule. Residents and water customers should watch for official LCRA updates and any local utility announcements before assuming their watering schedule has changed.

This is a November 19 editorial update based on reporting from earlier this week; it does not imply that every underlying event occurred today.
