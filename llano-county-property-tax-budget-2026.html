Llano County Tax Rate Stays Flat — But Bills Can Still Rise

County commissioners held the rate at $0.25953 per $100, while rising taxable values can increase the amount some owners owe.

Llano County commissioners approved the 2026–27 budget and kept the county tax rate at $0.25953 per $100 of taxable value. Local reporting noted that higher valuations can still result in larger tax bills. Residents should compare taxable value, exemptions and every taxing unit listed on their bill.
