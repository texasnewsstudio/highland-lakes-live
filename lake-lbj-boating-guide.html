Lake LBJ Boating: What to Check Before You Go

A practical guide to lake conditions, safety and planning a day on the water.

Lake LBJ is a popular destination for boating, fishing and waterfront recreation in the Highland Lakes region. Conditions can change with weather, water releases and heavy weekend traffic, so planning ahead is worthwhile.

Before launching, review current lake information from the Lower Colorado River Authority and check National Weather Service forecasts. Thunderstorms can develop quickly in Central Texas, bringing lightning and gusty winds even when the morning starts clear.

Boaters should verify public access points and any local restrictions, carry properly sized life jackets, and share their route and expected return time. A good lake day begins with reliable information, not assumptions about yesterday’s conditions.
