MARBLE FALLS WALKWAY OF LIGHTS SET TO OPEN NOVEMBER 20

The city's holiday display returns Friday with new features and free admission.

Marble Falls is preparing to welcome visitors back to its Walkway of Lights on Friday, November 20. Local reporting published November 17 says the seasonal attraction will feature new displays and remain free to enter, giving families another option for an evening outing as the Thanksgiving period approaches.

The return of the lights is a significant part of the city's late-fall visitor calendar. For downtown merchants and restaurants, the attraction also creates an opportunity to welcome guests who may combine a walk through the display with shopping or dinner in Marble Falls. That economic benefit is a potential effect, not a measured projection.

Anyone planning to attend should confirm the latest opening schedule and any weather-related changes with the event organizers before leaving home. The opening date is November 20; the display is not being described as already open on this November 18 update.
