Marble Falls Approves Funding for New Walkway of Lights Displays

Marble Falls has approved funding for new light sculptures planned for the annual Walkway of Lights holiday attraction.

The decision concerns a signature seasonal event that draws visitors downtown and along the waterfront. The approved spending will support new holiday displays, according to local reporting published October 23.
