Highland Lakes Weather: Pleasant Tuesday, Rain Chances Build Late in the Week

October 27, 2026 · Highland Lakes Live Weather · Powered by Texas News Studio

The Highland Lakes are enjoying a fairly comfortable Tuesday, with partly sunny skies and afternoon temperatures generally in the lower 80s. Whether you're in Marble Falls, Burnet, Kingsland or Horseshoe Bay, it's a good day to get outside before the weather turns a little more unsettled.

Wednesday looks warm again, with sunshine for much of the day. A stray late-day shower or thunderstorm is possible, but widespread rain is not the main story yet.

Thursday is when the pattern begins changing. Clouds increase and scattered showers become more likely across the region. Friday brings the better opportunity for rain, with some thunderstorms possible and cooler afternoon temperatures in the 70s.

The weekend looks mixed: a few showers or storms could linger Saturday, followed by a generally quieter and warmer Sunday. Forecast details can change, especially from one lake community to another, so keep an eye on the latest National Weather Service updates.
