QUARRY PARK LIGHTS UP! GRANITE SHOALS KICKS OFF HOLIDAY MAGIC

Christmas at Quarry Park begins Saturday evening with seasonal lights.

Granite Shoals is scheduled to launch Christmas at Quarry Park on Saturday, November 21, at 6 p.m. The seasonal display is listed at Quarry Park, 2221 N. Phillips Ranch Road, and is scheduled to continue into early January.

The event gives families a local option for an evening stroll among holiday lights. Visitors should check the organizer's latest information for access, weather adjustments and any special programming.
