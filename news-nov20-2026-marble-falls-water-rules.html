WATER RULE SHAKE-UP! MARBLE FALLS CHANGES RESTRICTIONS

A new city water-restriction update has residents checking what the rules mean for outdoor use.

Marble Falls has changed its water restrictions, according to local reporting dated November 19. For households and businesses, the practical question is what outdoor watering is permitted under the city's latest rules.

Restrictions can depend on the city's current drought stage, the type of water use and the property's location. Residents should check the city's published water conservation notices for the precise schedule and any exceptions rather than rely on older guidance.

This Highland Lakes Live update highlights the reported policy change. It does not reproduce a watering schedule that has not been independently confirmed.
