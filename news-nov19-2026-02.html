MARBLE FALLS' NEXT BIG MAKEOVER? RESIDENTS ASKED TO SHAPE CITY PARKS

City seeks public input on the future of its parks and outdoor spaces.

Marble Falls officials are asking residents to take part in a community survey about the city's parks, according to reporting published November 18.

The survey offers residents a way to weigh in on recreational priorities and the types of improvements they would like considered. No specific project or funding outcome is guaranteed by the survey alone.
