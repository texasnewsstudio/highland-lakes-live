Burnet County Prepares for Tuesday Election Day Voting

November 2, 2026 · Highland Lakes Live

Burnet County residents who did not vote early have their next opportunity on Tuesday, November 3, when Election Day polls are scheduled to open from 7 a.m. to 7 p.m.

The early-voting period ended Friday, October 30. Local reporting identifies Election Day polling locations across Burnet County, with election-day voting available at designated sites. Residents should confirm their assigned or eligible polling location and registration details before traveling.

The election includes state and national races. Llano County voters should consult Llano County election information rather than assume Burnet County voting locations apply to them. No election results are available before voting concludes.
