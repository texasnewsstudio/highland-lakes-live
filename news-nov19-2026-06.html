RIVER CROSSING DRAMA! KINGSLAND SLAB STILL CLOSED AFTER HIGH WATER

Strong Llano River flow has kept a familiar low-water crossing shut.

The Kingsland Slab crossing on County Road 307 remained closed as of the latest local report dated November 16, after heavy rain and elevated Llano River levels.

The closure was originally imposed on November 9. Because conditions can change, this November 19 roundup does not assert the crossing is still closed today; drivers should check Llano County's latest road status before traveling.

This is a November 19 editorial update based on reporting from earlier this week; it does not imply that every underlying event occurred today.
