FIESTAJAM SEEKS A BIGGER ROLE FOR MARBLE FALLS SONGWRITERS

Festival organizers want the Highland Lakes to become a stronger destination for Texas songwriting.

Following its November festival, FiestaJAM organizers are working to elevate Marble Falls as a destination for songwriters and live music. The nonprofit festival supports regional music and youth music education. The ambition to become a songwriting hub is a goal expressed by organizers, not an official designation.
