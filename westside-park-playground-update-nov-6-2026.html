Westside Park Playground Project Faces Higher Price Tag

November 6, 2026 · Highland Lakes Live News Desk

Marble Falls officials are moving forward with plans for an inclusive playground despite increased project costs.

The planned inclusive playground at Marble Falls’ Westside Park has a higher projected cost, according to local reporting published November 4. The playground is part of a larger park improvement effort, with a completion timetable now discussed by project officials. For families, the key questions are the final construction schedule, accessibility features and how the city plans to manage the additional cost. Residents can follow city updates for confirmed milestones.
