FREE THANKSGIVING MEALS TODAY! FOOD PANTRY OPENS AT 11

Joseph's Food Pantry plans to distribute holiday meals while supplies last.

Joseph's Food Pantry at 8200 RR 1431 in Granite Shoals is scheduled to provide Thanksgiving meals beginning at 11 a.m. Saturday, November 21. The distribution is planned to continue until supplies run out, with local reporting suggesting that is often around 1 p.m.

Anyone planning to attend should arrive early and confirm any eligibility or pickup details directly with the pantry. The offering is one of several free holiday meal efforts scheduled across the Highland Lakes.
