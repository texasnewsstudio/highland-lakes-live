LLANO RIVER RISE SHOWS HOW FAST LOW-WATER CROSSINGS CHANGE

Published November 15, 2026 · Highland Lakes Live

Autumn rainfall sent the Llano River higher and made some low-water crossings impassable.

DailyTrib reported November 10 that the river rose several feet overnight following rain. The event illustrates why a crossing that is passable one day can become dangerous within hours after upstream storms.

Never drive around barricades or enter flowing water over a road. River levels and road closures are time-sensitive; check local authorities and current gauges rather than relying on an earlier news account.
