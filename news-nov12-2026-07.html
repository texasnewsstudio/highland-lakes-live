RECENT LLANO RIVER RISE SHOWS WHY CROSSINGS NEED CAUTION

Rain upstream sent the river higher this week and affected low-water crossings.

The Llano River rose sharply after upstream rainfall earlier this week. Local reporting described multiple impassable low-water crossings on November 10. Conditions can change rapidly, and a crossing that was hazardous earlier in the week may have a different status now. Drivers should use current official road information and never drive through water over a roadway.
