Burnet Residents Prepare for November Waste Collection Change

Burnet County · October 27, 2026

Burnet is preparing for a change in its waste collection provider beginning in November. City officials have been sharing information about the transition, including how existing carts will be removed and new carts delivered.

Collection zones and pickup schedules may also change, making it important for households to check their assigned service days rather than assume the old schedule will continue.

Residents should use the City of Burnet's official communications to confirm their cart delivery and collection details.
