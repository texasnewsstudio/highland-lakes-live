RIVER CONDITIONS • NOVEMBER 10, 2026

LLANO RIVER RISE PROMPTS FLOOD WATCHFULNESS

Recent upstream rain could produce minor flooding along the Llano River on November 10–11.

Several inches of rain across the upper Llano River basin have increased the potential for minor flooding downstream during Tuesday and Wednesday, November 10–11. The forecast was reported Monday, November 9.

For residents and visitors, the practical concern is not simply how much rain falls at home. River levels can rise after upstream rainfall, even where local skies are clearing. Low-water crossings and river access points deserve particular attention.

People planning to travel near the Llano River should check current National Weather Service river forecasts and local road closure notices before setting out. A forecast for minor flooding does not mean every neighborhood or crossing will flood.

Reporting basis: November 9 local reporting. Highland Lakes Live update: November 10, 2026.
