BURNET BAND CLOSES ITS MARCHING SEASON AT STATE CONTEST

November 14, 2026 · Regional news roundup

The Esprit De Corps finishes a competitive fall season.

The Burnet High School Esprit De Corps concluded its marching competition season with a state contest appearance, according to DailyTrib reporting dated November 12. The appearance reflects months of rehearsals and performances by student musicians and staff. This article recaps the achievement rather than announcing a new competition on November 14.
