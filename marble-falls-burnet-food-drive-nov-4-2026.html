Marble Falls and Burnet Food Drive Returns Wednesday, November 4

November 4, 2026 · Highland Lakes Live

A regional food drive is scheduled for Wednesday, November 4, giving Highland Lakes residents an opportunity to support neighbors through a seasonal community collection effort.

The Picayune Magazine and KBEY 103.9 FM Radio Picayune are organizing the annual drive at the Marble Falls and Burnet fire stations. Organizers have announced a collection window of 6 a.m. to 3 p.m. Wednesday.

The two-city event brings together residents from across the Highland Lakes area. Those planning to contribute can check the organizers’ latest announcements for requested donations and any instructions before visiting a collection site.
