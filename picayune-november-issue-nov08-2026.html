NOVEMBER PICAYUNE SPOTLIGHTS HIGHLAND LAKES PEOPLE

The newest issue of a familiar regional magazine puts local stories back in the spotlight as the holiday season approaches.

November 8, 2026

A fresh November issue of The Picayune Magazine is circulating in the Highland Lakes, and it offers a reminder that not every meaningful regional story begins with a siren, a storm or a government vote.

Local reporting published November 6 announced the new issue and described coverage that includes an Army-related feature. For residents who have watched the area change over the years, these profiles can provide a different kind of community record: the people, organizations and traditions behind the daily headlines.

Community magazines also serve a practical purpose for newcomers. Someone who has recently moved to Marble Falls, Burnet, Kingsland or a lakeside neighborhood may know the major roads and stores but still be learning how the region fits together. Long-form local features can fill in that picture.

The issue’s publication is the news here; Highland Lakes Live is not presenting its contents as our own reporting. Readers interested in the complete features should obtain the magazine or visit the publisher’s website.

As the area grows, the value of documenting local history grows with it. New construction and changing demographics can alter the landscape quickly, but stories about longtime residents and community institutions help explain what makes the Highland Lakes more than a collection of lakefront subdivisions.
