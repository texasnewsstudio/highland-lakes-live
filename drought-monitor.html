What the drought categories mean

CategoryPlain-language meaningWhat to watch locally
D0Abnormally dryPasture stress and drying ponds may begin
D1Moderate droughtReduced forage growth and increased water demand
D2Severe droughtMore serious pasture, stock-water and crop impacts
D3Extreme droughtMajor agricultural and water-supply stress
D4Exceptional droughtWidespread, exceptional long-term impacts

The U.S. Drought Monitor combines precipitation, soil moisture, streamflow, vegetation and expert input to map drought intensity. It is released each Thursday using conditions valid through Tuesday morning, so the publication date and the valid date are different. For ranchers, D0 can be an early warning; D1–D2 may signal increasing pressure on forage and stock water; D3–D4 indicate increasingly severe impacts. A county can contain more than one category, so the map should be checked at the property level where possible. Drought Monitor categories are not a real-time measurement of a particular pond, well or pasture.
