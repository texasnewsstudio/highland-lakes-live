Fiestajam Weekend Brings Live Music To The Highland Lakes

November 6, 2026 · Highland Lakes Live News Desk

The November 6–8 songwriter showcase and concert series features regional and nationally known performers.

FiestaJAM returns to the Highland Lakes November 6–8 with songwriter showcases and concerts. Organizers have promoted a lineup that includes Diamond Rio, Gary P. Nunn, and Ruben Ramos & The Mexican Revolution. The weekend offers residents and visitors a chance to hear a mix of country, roots and regional music. Before traveling, attendees should confirm performance schedules, locations, ticket availability and any weather-related changes with festival organizers.
