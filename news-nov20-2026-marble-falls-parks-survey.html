PARKS MAKEOVER? MARBLE FALLS ASKS RESIDENTS TO SHAPE WHAT'S NEXT

The city is seeking community feedback about its parks and outdoor spaces.

Marble Falls is asking residents to complete a community survey about the future of the city's parks, according to local reporting dated November 18.

The survey gives residents a chance to identify priorities for recreation and public outdoor spaces before future planning decisions are made. Asking for input does not mean any particular project has been approved.

The central question for residents is which park improvements they want city leaders to consider. People interested in participating should check the city's official channels for survey access and deadlines.
