Burnet Volleyball Closes Historic Season With Playoff Title

November 6, 2026 · Highland Lakes Live News Desk

The Lady Dawgs won a bi-district championship before falling to Tivy in the area round.

Burnet High School volleyball ended a memorable season as bi-district champions. The Lady Dawgs finished an undefeated district campaign and advanced in the postseason before their run ended against Tivy High School in the area round. The result closes a season defined by consistency and a championship achievement. The team’s district success and playoff victory provide a strong foundation for returning players and the program.
