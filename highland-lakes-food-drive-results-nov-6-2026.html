Food Drive Brings In Thousands Of Pounds For Local Families

November 6, 2026 · Highland Lakes Live News Desk

The November 4 Picayune–KBEY campaign collected thousands of pounds of food and nearly $5,000 for area pantries.

Organizers reported a strong finish to the 15th annual Picayune–KBEY Food Drive. The November 4 effort collected thousands of pounds of nonperishable goods along with nearly $5,000 in monetary donations. The annual event brings together residents, volunteers and community organizations to support food pantries serving families in Marble Falls, Burnet and surrounding communities. For households looking to help beyond the one-day drive, contacting local food pantries about their most-needed items is a practical next step.
