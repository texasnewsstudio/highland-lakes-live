Hill Country livestock market notes

For ranchers around Burnet, Llano, Kingsland and the surrounding Hill Country, a statewide auction summary is a useful temperature check, but it cannot replace the price sheet from the sale barn where a particular load is marketed. Weight class, condition, breed, health, weaning history, lot size and buyer competition all affect the final bid. This page therefore puts local sale reports first and uses the USDA statewide summary only as broader context when the underlying report is available. No Burnet- or Llano-specific sale-barn figure is published here until a dated report can be verified. Hay prices are also left blank rather than inferred from a statewide average.

Statewide USDA summaries provide a useful market benchmark, but they do not represent the price a producer will receive at a particular Burnet or Llano area auction. Local prices depend on weight, grade, condition, shrink, lot size and the buyers present. This page now embeds the frequently refreshed Texas cattle market dashboard and the original USDA hay-report feed so visitors can inspect the latest published reporting dates directly. The source report may update weekly or biweekly; it is not a continuous real-time auction feed.
