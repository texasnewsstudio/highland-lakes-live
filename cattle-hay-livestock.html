What this means for Hill Country producers

These are the latest statewide USDA market reports available as of November 11, 2026. The cattle report summarizes auction receipts and prices by weight and grade; it does not represent a guaranteed bid at any local sale. The hay report is even more limited this week: it records no confirmed trades, so the published prices are asking prices only. We will not substitute statewide or South Texas numbers for Burnet or Llano quotes when the agency does not publish county-level results.

USDA publishes the cattle summary weekly and the Texas direct hay report weekly or biweekly depending on market activity. The figures on this page should be refreshed when the agency posts a new report. For local transactions, check directly with the sale barn or hay supplier.
