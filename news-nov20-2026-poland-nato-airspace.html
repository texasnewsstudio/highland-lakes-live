POLAND SOUNDS NATO ALARM! RUSSIAN MISSILES AND DRONE BREACH AIRSPACE

Warsaw says three Russian objects entered Polish airspace and vows to seek stronger NATO protection.

Polish officials said on November 20 that three objects from Russia entered the country's airspace overnight. Defence Minister Wladyslaw Kosiniak-Kamysz described the objects as likely two missiles and a jet-powered drone, calling the incident a provocation.

Poland says it will seek additional NATO support to protect the alliance's eastern flank. The reported incursions coincided with military exercises involving Ukraine's European allies. Officials were investigating the locations where objects were reported to have landed.

The reports do not establish that NATO has invoked collective defence or that the alliance is at war with Russia. The immediate development is Poland's demand for greater support and an ongoing investigation.
