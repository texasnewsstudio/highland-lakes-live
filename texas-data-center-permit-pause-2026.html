Texas Data Center Pause Puts Water and Power Under Scrutiny

A state permitting pause and new reporting requirements put electricity, water consumption and infrastructure costs in the spotlight.

On September 21, Governor Greg Abbott directed TCEQ to halt permits sought by data centers pending an ERCOT audit. The state also called for greater reporting on water use, electricity demand and community impacts. The order is statewide; it does not establish that any particular Highland Lakes project has been proposed or approved.
