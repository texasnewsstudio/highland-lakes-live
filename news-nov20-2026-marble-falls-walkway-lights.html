MARBLE FALLS · NOVEMBER 20, 2026 · REGIONAL NEWS

TONIGHT'S BIG SWITCH-ON! WALKWAY OF LIGHTS RETURNS FOR FREE

The 36th annual display is scheduled to open November 20 at Lakeside Park.

Marble Falls' Walkway of Lights is scheduled to open Friday, November 20, from 6 to 10 p.m. at Lakeside Park and Beach, 305 Buena Vista Drive.

The free holiday attraction is celebrating its 36th year. Organizers say the display will operate nightly through January 2, weather permitting, with Santa accepting Christmas wishes during the season.

Wet conditions can force a closure. Organizers advise checking their official updates before traveling, particularly on rainy days.
