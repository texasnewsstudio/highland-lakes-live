FALL AND WINTER GUIDE OPENS UP MORE HIGHLAND LAKES DAY TRIPS

A new seasonal activity guide gives residents ideas for exploring the region as temperatures cool and holiday schedules fill up.

November 8, 2026

Cooler weather changes the way people use the Highland Lakes. The midday heat becomes less of an obstacle, outdoor walks feel more inviting and towns begin shifting from summer lake traffic toward festivals and holiday events.

The 2026 fall/winter edition of 101 Fun Things to do in the Highland Lakes was announced in early October, offering another resource for families who want local activities without making a long trip to Austin or San Antonio.

The appeal is not limited to visitors. Residents can use seasonal guides to rediscover nearby parks, historic sites and downtown districts that are easy to overlook during the routines of work and errands.

Planning still matters. State park access, event schedules and business hours can vary by day and season. For outdoor excursions, check the forecast and remember that sunset arrives earlier after the fall time change.

For the region’s small businesses, local day trips can be important. Spending an afternoon in a neighboring town may support restaurants, shops and attractions that depend on both residents and travelers.
