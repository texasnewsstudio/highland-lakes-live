BURNET MAIL-SCAM WARNING PUTS RESIDENTS ON ALERT

Published November 15, 2026 · Highland Lakes Live

Burnet officials have warned residents about suspicious mail seeking payments.

A November 10 DailyTrib report says the city alerted residents to a mail scam that appeared to demand additional payments. Official-looking correspondence can be confusing, particularly when it references government services or urgent deadlines.

Residents should verify unexpected payment requests using contact details from an official city website or a known statement, rather than phone numbers or links printed in a suspicious letter. Avoid sending personal information until the request is authenticated.
