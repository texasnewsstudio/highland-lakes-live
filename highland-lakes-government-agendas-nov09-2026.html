THIS WEEK’S LOCAL MEETINGS PUT ROADS, WATER AND FUNDING ON THE AGENDA

Llano and Burnet county officials and area city councils have several practical decisions scheduled this week.

Local government meetings across the Highland Lakes region this week cover topics ranging from animal-welfare funding to water infrastructure and road equipment.

Llano County Commissioners Court scheduled its regular meeting for 9 a.m. Monday, November 9, at the Llano County Law Enforcement Center, 752 Andy Taylor Drive. Listed discussion items include proposed funding for Hill Country Humane Society improvements, the county investment policy and a donation for the Kingsland Library.

Burnet County Commissioners Court is scheduled for 9 a.m. Tuesday, November 10, at the county courthouse in Burnet. The published agenda summary includes a Silver Creek Village replat, dump-truck leasing and IT infrastructure funding.

Bertram City Council is scheduled to meet at noon Tuesday, with water-line expansion and staffing among its listed topics. Burnet City Council is scheduled for 5 p.m. Tuesday, including flood-maintenance funding and a grid-resiliency grant discussion.

These are scheduled agenda items, not decisions already made. Residents should check the latest official postings for amendments, cancellations or supporting documents before attending.
