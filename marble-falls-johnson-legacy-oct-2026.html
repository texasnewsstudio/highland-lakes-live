Marble Falls Community Celebrates the Johnson Family Legacy

More than 100 people gathered at Harmony Park on October 23 to remember Ross and Alice Johnson and their contributions to Marble Falls.

The gathering brought neighbors together to reflect on the family’s business history and influence on the community. Local reporting described the Johnsons’ story as closely connected with the development and identity of Marble Falls.

The event underscores the role of long-established families and local businesses in the Highland Lakes region.

Reporting basis: DailyTrib local reporting, October 27, 2026. Original summary by Highland Lakes Live.
