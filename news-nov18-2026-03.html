NEW MARBLE FALLS HIGH SCHOOL BASEBALL FIELD NEARS FINISH LINE

Scearce Field improvements are approaching completion ahead of the 2027 season.

Construction at Scearce Field, the Marble Falls High School baseball facility, is approaching completion. Reporting published November 17 says the improved field is nearly ready for the Mustangs' 2027 baseball season after work that began earlier in 2026.

The project is an investment in the school's athletic facilities and in the experience of players, coaches and spectators. A completed venue can also support practices and home games, although the school will determine the final schedule and when the facilities can be used.

For families following the Mustangs, the next milestone is confirmation of the field's readiness and the 2027 baseball calendar. Near completion should not be confused with a formal opening announcement.
