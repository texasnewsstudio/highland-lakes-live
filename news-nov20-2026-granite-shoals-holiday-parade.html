GRANITE SHOALS · NOVEMBER 20, 2026 · REGIONAL NEWS

CHRISTMAS ON WHEELS! GOLF CART PARADE SET TO LIGHT UP QUARRY PARK

Granite Shoals plans a November 21 celebration with a golf-cart parade and tree lighting.

Granite Shoals is preparing for its annual Christmas celebration on Saturday, November 21, according to local reporting published November 17.

The planned festivities include a golf-cart parade, tree-lighting ceremony and holiday activities at Quarry Park. The gathering brings seasonal celebrations to the lakeside community ahead of Thanksgiving.

This is a preview of a scheduled event, not confirmation that it has already taken place. Visitors should consult local organizers for any last-minute schedule changes.
