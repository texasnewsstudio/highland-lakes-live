Horseshoe Bay Seeks Residents to Serve on City Boards

Horseshoe Bay is inviting residents to apply for openings on seven city boards, commissions and committees.

The openings offer residents an opportunity to participate in local decisions and civic oversight. People interested in serving should review the city’s current notices for board duties, eligibility and application procedures.

The vacancies were reported October 27. Residents should confirm which positions remain available before applying.

Reporting basis: DailyTrib local reporting, October 27, 2026. Original summary by Highland Lakes Live.
