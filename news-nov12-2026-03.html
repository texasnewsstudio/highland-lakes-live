MARBLE FALLS HOSPITAL TESTS ITS MASS-CASUALTY RESPONSE

A scheduled November 12 drill puts emergency coordination and readiness to the test.

Baylor Scott & White Medical Center in Marble Falls is conducting a planned mass-casualty exercise on November 12. Such drills allow healthcare teams and emergency partners to practice how they would respond if many patients arrived at once. The activity is a scheduled exercise rather than an actual emergency.
