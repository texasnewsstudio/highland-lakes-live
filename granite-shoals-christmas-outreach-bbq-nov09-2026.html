GRANITE SHOALS BBQ FUNDRAISER AIMS TO HELP FAMILIES THIS CHRISTMAS

A November 14 barbecue at the fire station will raise money for local holiday outreach.

Granite Shoals residents have a straightforward way to support local holiday outreach this weekend: buy a barbecue plate at the city fire station.

The Granite Shoals Christmas Outreach fundraiser is scheduled for Saturday, November 14, beginning at 11 a.m. at the fire station, 8410 RM 1431. Organizers listed plates at $20, with food prepared by the Granite Shoals Police Officers Association and Granite Shoals Fire Auxiliary.

The event also plans a raffle for two whole briskets, with tickets priced at $5 each. Organizers told local reporters that food commonly sells out by 2 p.m., so early arrival is encouraged.

The fundraiser illustrates how volunteer groups and local public-safety organizations contribute beyond their regular duties. Proceeds support Christmas outreach, and event details should be confirmed with the organizers before traveling.
