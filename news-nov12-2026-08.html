BURNET WARNS RESIDENTS TO QUESTION SUSPICIOUS MAIL

City officials are urging residents to verify unexpected payment demands before responding.

Burnet officials have warned about misleading mail solicitations that appear to demand extra payments. Residents receiving unexpected notices should verify the sender and contact the city through its published contact channels rather than relying on numbers or links printed in a suspicious letter.
