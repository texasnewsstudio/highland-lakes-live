BURNET COUNTY ASKS RESIDENTS TO HELP SHAPE THE NEXT ROAD MAP

A series of public meetings gives drivers a chance to weigh in on future road priorities.

Burnet County residents have a chance to influence which transportation problems get attention next. Public meetings announced November 13 invite residents to discuss infrastructure needs and future priorities. That matters on roads used by commuters, school buses and lake visitors alike. The meetings are an opportunity for input, not an announcement that any specific widening or new road has been funded.

Why this matters locally

The Highland Lakes region is changing, and decisions about infrastructure, schools, public services and community life affect residents across city boundaries. Highland Lakes Live will distinguish scheduled actions and reported developments from final outcomes.
