$1,000 BOOST! KINGSLAND CHAMBER UNVEILS GRANTS FOR LOCAL BUSINESSES

Three chamber members could receive marketing or appearance grants.

The Kingsland/Lake LBJ Chamber of Commerce has launched the Growing Together Grants Program, which plans to award three grants of $1,000 each to eligible chamber members.

According to reporting from November 16, the money is intended to help with business appearance or marketing. Applications are due January 15, 2027, and applicants should confirm eligibility and submission instructions with the chamber.

This is a November 19 editorial update based on reporting from earlier this week; it does not imply that every underlying event occurred today.
