SMALL CITY, BIG BEAUTY! GRANITE SHOALS WINS STATEWIDE HONOR

Granite Shoals earns silver recognition for its scenic qualities.

Granite Shoals has received silver certification through Scenic Texas, becoming a first-time recipient of the statewide recognition, according to November 18 reporting.

The honor celebrates the community's scenic appeal. It is an award, not an announcement of new zoning restrictions or a major development project.

This is a November 19 editorial update based on reporting from earlier this week; it does not imply that every underlying event occurred today.
