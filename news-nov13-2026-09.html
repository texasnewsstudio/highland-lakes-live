MARBLE FALLS MUSEUM PREPARES EXHIBIT ON GERMAN SETTLERS

Family artifacts and historic records will tell stories of early Hill Country communities.

HIGHLAND LAKES LIVE · Original report or event listing: October 15, 2026

The Falls on the Colorado Museum in Marble Falls is preparing new second-floor exhibits focused in part on German settlers and ranching history. Families have shared artifacts and memoirs that document life in the region during the nineteenth century. The museum announced a December 12 opening for the new exhibits; the opening has not yet taken place.
