SMALL CITY, BIG HONOR! GRANITE SHOALS SNAGS STATEWIDE BEAUTY AWARD

Scenic Texas has awarded Granite Shoals silver certification, recognizing the city's scenic qualities.

Granite Shoals has earned silver certification from Scenic Texas, becoming a first-time recipient of the statewide recognition, according to local reporting published November 18.

The recognition puts a spotlight on how the lakeside community presents and protects its surroundings. A silver certification is an award for scenic qualities; it is not a ranking of every Texas city.

For residents, the award offers a fresh talking point about Granite Shoals' identity as the Highland Lakes region continues to attract visitors and new neighbors.
