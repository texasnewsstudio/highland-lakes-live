Horseshoe Bay City Secretary Receives Statewide Recognition

Horseshoe Bay City Secretary Susie Quinn has been named the 2026 Texas Municipal Clerk of the Year.

The recognition highlights the administrative work that supports public meetings, official records and day-to-day city government. City secretaries often serve as a key point of contact for residents seeking municipal information.

The honor was reported October 27 and adds a statewide distinction to Horseshoe Bay’s local government office.
