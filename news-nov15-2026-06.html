BURNET VOLLEYBALL COACH EARNS SEASON HONOR

Published November 15, 2026 · Highland Lakes Live

Crystal Shipley received Coach of the Year recognition after a standout Burnet volleyball season.

DailyTrib reported November 11 that Burnet High School volleyball coach Crystal Shipley was recognized following a record-breaking campaign. Coaching honors often reflect the work of an entire program, including student-athletes and assistant coaches.

The recognition gives Burnet another opportunity to celebrate school athletics and the commitment required through a long season. Readers can consult the original local report for the award details.
