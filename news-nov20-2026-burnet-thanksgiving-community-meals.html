THANKSGIVING HELP IS COMING! FREE COMMUNITY FEASTS ON THE HORIZON

Local nonprofits and organizations are preparing holiday meals and festivities for November 26.

Thanksgiving falls on Thursday, November 26, and nonprofits and community groups across the Highland Lakes have announced free holiday festivities and meals, according to regional reporting published November 18.

The events can offer food and fellowship for people who may otherwise spend the holiday alone or face tight budgets. Each organizer sets its own location, schedule and participation details.

This report does not imply that every listed meal has unlimited capacity. Residents should confirm the latest details directly with the event organizer before traveling.
