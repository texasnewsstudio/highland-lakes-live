WESTERN SWING TAKES THE STAGE! TEXAS PLAYBOYS PLAY BERTRAM

The Globe Theatre hosts a Saturday-night show under Jason Roberts' direction.

The Globe Theatre in Bertram has Bob Wills Texas Playboys Under the Direction of Jason Roberts on its calendar for Saturday, November 21, from 8 to 10 p.m. The venue is at 132 W. Vaughan Street.

The performance gives Western-swing fans a reason to make the trip to Bertram. Attendees should confirm ticket availability and any last-minute changes with the theatre.
