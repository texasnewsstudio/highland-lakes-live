BURNET CLASSROOM IDEAS GET $70,000 BOOST FROM SPARK TANK

Published November 15, 2026 · Highland Lakes Live

A local education fundraiser brought in more than $70,000 for classroom innovation.

The Burnet Consolidated Independent School District Education Foundation raised more than $70,000 through its second annual Spark Tank fundraiser, according to a November 10 DailyTrib report. The event supports teachers seeking resources for creative learning projects.

The funding highlights a practical challenge for public schools: classroom ideas often need equipment, supplies or program support beyond ordinary budgets. Community fundraising can help bridge that gap, though individual awards and their classroom outcomes require follow-up.
