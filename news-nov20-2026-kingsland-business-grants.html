$1,000 ON THE TABLE! KINGSLAND BUSINESSES EYE NEW CHAMBER GRANTS

Three eligible chamber members could receive $1,000 for marketing or appearance improvements.

The Kingsland/Lake LBJ Chamber of Commerce has introduced its Growing Together Grants Program, which will award three $1,000 grants to chamber members, according to reporting dated November 16.

The money is intended to support improvements to business appearance or marketing. Applications are due January 15, 2027, giving eligible members time to prepare their proposals.

The program represents a modest but concrete opportunity for participating local businesses. The grants are competitive, not guaranteed payments to every applicant.
