BIG CHANGES AHEAD! LAND CLEARED FOR MAJOR DEVELOPMENT

Highland Lakes Live News Desk | November 25, 2026

Dozens of acres along Manzano Mile have been cleared for a major development, according to local reporting on November 24. The work marks a visible step forward, although clearing alone does not establish when construction will finish or when the development will open.

The planned Highlands development spans roughly 254 acres near FM 1431, opposite local schools. Plans include hundreds of detached homes alongside other housing types and commercial land.

What residents should watch

Road and utility work precedes home construction. The timing of individual homes and businesses remains uncertain.
