Hill Country Power Line Route Changed After Landowner Concerns

Regulators revised a proposed high-voltage transmission route as landowners raised questions about property impacts.

Texas utility regulators approved a revised route for the Howard–Solstice transmission line in September, emphasizing a path north of Uvalde and alignment with existing corridors where possible. Landowners have raised concerns about landscape, land use and the decision-making process. This is a broader Hill Country story, not a claim that the line crosses Horseshoe Bay.
