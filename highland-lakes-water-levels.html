Understanding Highland Lakes Water Levels

Why lake levels change and where to find official readings.

The Highland Lakes are a chain of reservoirs along the Colorado River in Central Texas. Water levels respond to rainfall, inflows, evaporation and water-management operations. Not every lake changes in the same way.

For current measurements, the Lower Colorado River Authority provides official lake information and Hydromet readings. These sources are more reliable than a screenshot or a social-media report that may already be out of date.

Anyone planning boating, shoreline work or a lakeside visit should check the specific lake rather than relying on a region-wide summary. Conditions can change after heavy rain or during extended dry periods.

Useful resources: LCRA · National Weather Service · DriveTexas
