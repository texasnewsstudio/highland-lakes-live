COTTONWOOD SHORES MOVES TOWARD OUTDOOR WARNING SIRENS

Two planned warning sirens would add another way to reach residents during dangerous weather.

Cottonwood Shores is planning to install two outdoor emergency sirens for severe-weather and other public-safety warnings. The planned system is intended to help reach people who may not be watching their phones or televisions. Sirens are only one part of a warning system, so residents should also keep emergency alerts enabled and follow official instructions.
