COTTONWOOD SHORES INSTALLING NEW WARNING SIREN SYSTEM

November 14, 2026 · Regional news roundup

Outdoor emergency notification improvements are underway.

DailyTrib reported November 11 that Cottonwood Shores is installing a warning siren system. Outdoor sirens are one component of public alerting and should complement, not replace, weather radio and phone alerts. Residents should consult city officials for installation status, activation procedures and coverage.
