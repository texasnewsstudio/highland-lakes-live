BURNET FLOOD BUYOUT PROGRAM REACHES EARLY MILESTONE

Published November 15, 2026 · Highland Lakes Live

Burnet completed its first five property closings under a flood-relief buyout program.

DailyTrib reported October 15 that five properties had closed under a multimillion-dollar flood buyout initiative. The program represents a longer-term approach to reducing repeated flood losses in vulnerable areas.

Buyout programs can help move households away from high-risk locations, but eligibility, timelines and funding requirements vary. Property owners should consult official city materials for current program details.
