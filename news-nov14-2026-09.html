TEXAS ROAD CREWS CONTINUE MONITORING FLOOD-AFFECTED ROUTES

November 14, 2026 · Regional news roundup

State emergency officials detail the response to recent storms.

In a November 13 state situation report, the Texas Division of Emergency Management said TxDOT continued monitoring weather, river rises and roads after flooding. The report noted 26 affected locations on November 9, including 18 road-segment closures, and later high-water closures on U.S. 377. These figures describe earlier conditions, not confirmed current closures on November 14. Travelers should consult TxDOT for live road status.
