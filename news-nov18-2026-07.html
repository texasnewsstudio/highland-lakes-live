HIGHLAND LAKES SQUADRON BRINGS 1940S STYLE TO HANGAR DANCE

A November 14 hangar gathering celebrated aviation history and community connections.

The Commemorative Air Force's Highland Lakes Squadron hosted a 1940s-themed hangar dinner and dance on Saturday, November 14, according to a local report published November 17. The gathering brought a period-inspired atmosphere to the regional aviation community.

Events of this kind connect the history of military and civilian aviation with people who may not otherwise visit a hangar or museum setting. Music, clothing and the hangar venue create a social way to encounter that history while supporting interest in local preservation activities.

The event has already taken place; this November 18 article is a recap rather than a listing for an upcoming dance. Readers interested in future activities can follow the squadron's announcements.
