Burnet County Property Taxes: What the New Rate Means

Burnet County adopted a $0.3541 property-tax rate per $100 in valuation for 2026. Here is what homeowners should check on their bills.

Burnet County’s September taxpayer impact statement estimated a $1,360.60 county tax bill for a median-value homestead at the proposed $0.3541 rate, compared with $1,297.80 the prior year. Commissioners subsequently adopted the $0.3541 rate. Individual bills vary with appraisals, exemptions, and other taxing entities.

For property-specific questions, check official documents and your applicable taxing or utility authorities. This report summarizes the cited public information and does not assert new local project approvals.
