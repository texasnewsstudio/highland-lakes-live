Llano City Council Meets November 2 as New Month Begins

November 2, 2026 · Highland Lakes Live

The City of Llano lists a City Council meeting for Monday, November 2, 2026, on its official public calendar. The meeting puts municipal business on the local agenda as the community enters November.

The city publishes council meeting information and agenda materials through its official website. Residents who want to follow city decisions, public comments, or specific agenda items should consult those materials for the meeting details.

The city calendar also lists another council meeting for November 16. No decisions from the November 2 meeting had been verified at publication time.
