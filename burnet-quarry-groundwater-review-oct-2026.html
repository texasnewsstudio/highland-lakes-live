Proposed Burnet County Quarry Faces Contested Groundwater Permit Review

A groundwater permit associated with a proposed 715-acre rock quarry in Burnet County has been designated a contested matter by the local groundwater district.

The designation sets the stage for additional review and hearings. The permit dispute matters to area residents concerned about groundwater use and industrial development, but it does not by itself establish the project’s final outcome.
