Fall Weather in the Texas Hill Country: What to Expect

Why autumn can bring both pleasant days and sudden storms.

Autumn in Central Texas often brings changing temperatures as cold fronts move south across the state. Warm afternoons can give way to cooler, breezy conditions, and some fronts can help trigger thunderstorms.

The exact timing and strength of each front require a current forecast. National Weather Service forecasts and alerts are the best sources for near-term decisions about outdoor plans.

Keep an eye on changing conditions if you are boating, camping or traveling. A seasonal weather pattern is not a prediction of a particular storm on a particular date.
