Highland Lakes Government Meetings: What to Check for the Week of November 9

Published November 7, 2026 · Highland Lakes Live

Newly published meeting-agenda listings give residents a chance to review local government business before next week.

Residents across Burnet and Llano counties can prepare for the next round of local government meetings by reviewing agendas for the week beginning Monday, November 9.

Local reporting published November 6 lists government meetings scheduled for the coming week. Specific times, locations and agenda items should be confirmed with each city or county government before attending.

For residents interested in roads, public safety, development or local spending, checking an agenda before a meeting is a useful way to find opportunities for public participation.
