LAKE BUCHANAN AND TRAVIS ABOVE NOVEMBER AVERAGES IN MORNING LCRA READINGS

November 18 lake-level data highlights differences across the Highland Lakes chain.

LCRA's November 18 readings at 4:55 a.m. showed Lake Buchanan at 1,018.53 feet above mean sea level, compared with a listed November average of 1,010.58 feet. Lake Travis stood at 681.71 feet, above its November average of 665.37 feet.

Other lakes were much closer to their listed seasonal benchmarks: Lake LBJ measured 824.41 feet against an 824.75-foot November average, while Lake Marble Falls measured 736.26 feet against 736.58 feet. These figures are point-in-time measurements, not forecasts.

Boaters should not use lake elevation alone to decide whether a ramp is usable. LCRA publishes ramp-specific minimum elevations and notes that access can be restricted during severe flooding. Always check the current ramp and safety notices before heading out.
