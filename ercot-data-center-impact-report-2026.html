ERCOT Seeks Data Center Details Ahead of December Report

Grid officials are gathering information about large data centers, including water use, grid power and neighborhood effects.

ERCOT requested information from developers of data centers of at least 25 megawatts pursuing grid connections. Topics include incentives, water sources, cooling systems, grid demand, traffic, noise and lighting. ERCOT says it plans to publish its findings on or before December 10, 2026.

For property-specific questions, check official documents and your applicable taxing or utility authorities. This report summarizes the cited public information and does not assert new local project approvals.
