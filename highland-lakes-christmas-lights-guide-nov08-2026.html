HIGHLAND LAKES CHRISTMAS LIGHTS GUIDE ARRIVES

A new regional holiday lights guide gives families a head start on planning December evenings across Burnet and Llano counties.

November 8, 2026

The Halloween decorations are coming down, but another season is already moving into view. The Picayune Magazine has released its 2026 Christmas Lights Guide, a free resource designed to help families find holiday activities and illuminated displays across the Highland Lakes.

The timing matters. November is when many families begin coordinating visits, school programs and weekend outings, and the region’s popular seasonal attractions can involve driving between communities. A consolidated guide can turn a scattered list of possibilities into an evening that actually works.

According to local reporting dated November 6, copies are available at businesses in Burnet and Llano counties. Readers should consult the current edition for confirmed dates and locations rather than rely on schedules from previous years. Holiday programs sometimes change their hours or admission arrangements.

There is a wider economic story behind the lights. Seasonal events draw visitors into downtown districts, restaurants and locally owned shops at a time when merchants are competing for holiday spending. Even free events can generate traffic for neighboring businesses.

For residents, the best approach is to pick a few destinations rather than trying to cover the entire region in one trip. Check the weather, confirm event hours and remember that a scenic drive can become a slow one when everyone has the same holiday plan.
