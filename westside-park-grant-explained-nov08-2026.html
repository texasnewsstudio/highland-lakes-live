WESTSIDE PARK GRANT MOVES IMPROVEMENTS FORWARD

Marble Falls accepted a $750,000 state parks grant, an important step toward long-discussed Westside Park improvements.

November 8, 2026

For Marble Falls families who have been waiting for improvements at Westside Park, one of the most important steps happened in a City Council meeting rather than on a construction site. The council formally accepted a $750,000 Texas Parks and Wildlife Department Local Park Grant in October.

The funding had been awarded earlier in 2026, but a grant award and a city’s formal acceptance are different stages of the process. By approving the agreement, the city moved the project closer to bidding and construction, according to an October 7 local report.

That distinction matters because public projects rarely move directly from announcement to finished playground. Design documents, bid specifications, contractor pricing and scheduling all affect the timeline. The public should watch for a bid notice or construction schedule before treating a projected completion date as guaranteed.

Westside Park has significance beyond any single piece of equipment. Public parks provide shared space for families who may not have large yards, and accessible amenities can make a difference for residents with mobility limitations and caregivers with young children.

One question worth following is whether the final construction price fits the available funding. The accepted grant provides substantial support, but it does not by itself establish the total cost of every improvement. The next documents released by the city should make the project’s scope and financial picture clearer.
