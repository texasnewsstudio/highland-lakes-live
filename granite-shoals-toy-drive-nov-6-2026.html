Holiday Toy Drive Seeks Donations For Granite Shoals Families

November 6, 2026 · Highland Lakes Live News Desk

Operation Fill the Boat is collecting unwrapped toys through November 30.

Operation Fill the Boat is back in Granite Shoals, collecting unwrapped toys through November 30 to help local families during the holiday season. The effort gives residents an opportunity to support children in their own community. Donors should check with organizers for current collection locations, accepted gifts and any age-group needs before dropping off items.
