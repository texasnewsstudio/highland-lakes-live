PEC YOUTH TOUR APPLICATION DEADLINE APPROACHES

Families across the Highland Lakes have until November 12 to explore a youth leadership opportunity through Pedernales Electric Cooperative.

November 8, 2026

For area high school students looking beyond the classroom, the calendar is getting tight. Pedernales Electric Cooperative is accepting applications for its 2027 Youth Tour, with a November 12 deadline identified in local community reporting. That leaves families only a few days to determine whether a student is eligible, gather the requested materials and submit an application.

The opportunity is especially relevant in a region where electric cooperatives are woven into daily life. PEC serves communities that stretch across the Hill Country, and its educational outreach offers young people a way to learn about public service, the electric industry and the decisions that shape their communities.

An application deadline is not the same thing as an award announcement. Students should review the cooperative’s current eligibility rules and required materials directly rather than assuming that every student in the service area qualifies. School counselors may also be able to help families plan around school schedules.

The practical takeaway is simple: November 12 is close enough that waiting until the last evening creates unnecessary risk. Students interested in the program can use the remaining days to confirm details, prepare thoughtful responses and make sure their submission is complete.

Highland Lakes Live will continue to watch for local educational opportunities with deadlines that can easily pass unnoticed amid school, work and holiday commitments.
