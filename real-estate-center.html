Before buying property in the Highland Lakes

Waterfront due diligence

Confirm floodplain status, dock permits, lake access, shoreline rules and flood insurance requirements for the exact property.

FEMA flood map search ↗

Taxes, HOA and utilities

Verify county appraisal records, special assessments, property owners' association restrictions, utilities and insurance costs before making an offer.

Understanding the figures

Median asking price measures advertised listings. It does not represent closed-sale price, appraised value, or a price estimate for an individual property.
