SUNRISE BEACH REMEMBERS LONGTIME POLICE OFFICER CLAYTON KLEEN

The lakeside community is mourning an officer who served its police department for a decade.

HIGHLAND LAKES LIVE · Original report or event listing: November 10, 2026

Sunrise Beach Police Officer Clayton Kleen died unexpectedly on November 10, according to local reporting. He had served the department for 10 years and previously worked with several other Central Texas law enforcement agencies. Police Chief Laurie Brock remembered his dedication and service to the community. The city and his colleagues are mourning his loss.
