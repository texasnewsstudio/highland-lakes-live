GRANITE SHOALS TOY DRIVE COLLECTS HOLIDAY GIFTS THROUGH NOVEMBER

Published November 15, 2026 · Highland Lakes Live

A Granite Shoals holiday toy drive is accepting donations for local families.

The Operation Fill the Boat toy drive returned in early November, according to DailyTrib's November 3 report. Organizers are collecting unwrapped toys through November 30 to support children in the community.

Community drives rely on both donated goods and volunteers to sort and distribute them. Prospective donors should check the organizer's current collection locations and any age or item guidelines before dropping off gifts.
