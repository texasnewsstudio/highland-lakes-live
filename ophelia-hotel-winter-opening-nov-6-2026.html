Marble Falls Boutique Hotel Project Targets Winter Opening

November 6, 2026 · Highland Lakes Live News Desk

The 127-room Ophelia hotel and conference center could open this winter, according to late-October reporting.

The Ophelia, a planned 127-room boutique hotel and conference center in Marble Falls, was reported in late October to be on track for a possible winter opening. The project could add visitor accommodations and meeting space to the city. An anticipated opening window is not a confirmed opening date; travelers and event planners should check directly with the property for reservations and operational updates.
