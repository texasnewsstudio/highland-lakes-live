MARBLE FALLS FACES A $300,000 WATER-PLANT DECISION TUESDAY

Council members are scheduled to consider reallocating funds for a water plant.

The Marble Falls City Council agenda for Tuesday, November 17 includes a proposed $300,000 water-plant reallocation, a Thunder Rock irrigation well project and a request for operators of city-owned property at 120 South Main Street. These are scheduled discussion or action items, not approved decisions. The outcome will depend on the council meeting.

Why this matters locally

The Highland Lakes region is changing, and decisions about infrastructure, schools, public services and community life affect residents across city boundaries. Highland Lakes Live will distinguish scheduled actions and reported developments from final outcomes.
