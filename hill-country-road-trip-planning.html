Planning a Highland Lakes Road Trip

A practical route-planning checklist for the Hill Country.

The Highland Lakes region connects communities including Marble Falls, Burnet, Kingsland, Llano and Horseshoe Bay. A short drive can include waterfront scenery, small-town shopping and outdoor recreation.

Before heading out, check road conditions through TxDOT DriveTexas and review the weather forecast. Rural routes may have limited services, and low-water crossings require special caution during heavy rain.

Choose a few stops rather than packing too much into one day. Confirm hours for parks, businesses and attractions directly, especially on holidays and weekends.

Useful resources: LCRA · National Weather Service · DriveTexas
