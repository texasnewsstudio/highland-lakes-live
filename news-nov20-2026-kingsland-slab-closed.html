ROAD STILL SHUT! FLOODWATERS KEEP KINGSLAND SLAB CLOSED

The low-water crossing remained closed November 19 after the Llano River's recent rise.

The Kingsland Slab, the low-water crossing on County Road 307, remained closed as of Thursday, November 19, according to local reporting. The crossing was shut after the Llano River rose during the November 9 flooding.

Although the river had receded, water was still reported over the crossing on Thursday morning. The route is under the jurisdiction of the Texas Department of Transportation.

The November 19 closure report is the latest confirmed status cited here; it is not a real-time road-opening notice for November 20. Drivers should check official TxDOT road conditions before attempting the crossing, and never drive around barricades.
