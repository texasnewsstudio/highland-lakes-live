COURTHOUSE SQUARE COMES ALIVE! LLANO MARKET RETURNS SATURDAY

Farmers, makers and crafters are scheduled to gather from 9 a.m. to 1 p.m.

Llano's Farmers and Crafters Market is listed for Saturday, November 21, from 9 a.m. until 1 p.m. on the courthouse square. The event focuses on homegrown and handmade goods, including food and crafts.

The morning market offers a chance to browse local products while spending time downtown. Availability varies by vendor, so visitors should not assume any particular product will be on sale.
