JAILHOUSE LAWSUIT SHOCK! FORMER INMATE ALLEGES ABUSE BEHIND BARS

Federal civil lawsuit raises serious allegations involving a former Burnet County jailer.

A former Burnet County Jail detainee has filed a federal lawsuit against the county and a former jailer, alleging sexual assault and coercion while she was incarcerated.

The claims are allegations made in a civil lawsuit, not findings of liability. Local reporting published November 18 says the former jailer is no longer employed at the facility and previously received two felony convictions involving misconduct with another inmate. Those earlier convictions should not be conflated with the new plaintiff's allegations.
