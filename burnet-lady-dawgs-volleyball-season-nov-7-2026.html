Burnet Lady Dawgs End Historic Volleyball Run After District and Bi-District Titles

Published November 7, 2026 · Highland Lakes Live

Burnet’s volleyball team followed an undefeated district campaign with a bi-district championship before falling to Tivy.

Burnet High School’s Lady Dawgs have completed a memorable 2026 volleyball season after an undefeated district run and a bi-district championship.

The team advanced to the area round before falling to Tivy High School, according to Highland Lakes reporting published November 5.

The finish marks an important achievement for Burnet athletes and provides a strong foundation for the program heading into the next season.
