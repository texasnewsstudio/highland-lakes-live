HORSESHOE BAY COUNCIL TO CHOOSE MAYOR PRO TEM

Tuesday's agenda also includes the city's 2027 holiday schedule.

Horseshoe Bay City Council is scheduled to meet Tuesday, November 17 at 3 p.m. Agenda items include choosing a mayor pro tem, setting the 2027 city holiday schedule and designating an official newspaper. None of those agenda items should be described as decided before the vote. Residents can review the agenda or attend the meeting for details.

Why this matters locally

The Highland Lakes region is changing, and decisions about infrastructure, schools, public services and community life affect residents across city boundaries. Highland Lakes Live will distinguish scheduled actions and reported developments from final outcomes.
