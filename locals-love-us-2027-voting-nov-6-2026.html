Highland Lakes Favorites Contest Opens For 2027 Voting

November 6, 2026 · Highland Lakes Live News Desk

The annual Locals Love Us program is inviting the community to recognize favorite businesses and services.

Voting has opened for the 2027 Locals Love Us contest, an annual community recognition program run by The Picayune and KBEY 103.9 FM. The program highlights businesses and services that residents value across the Highland Lakes. Anyone planning to participate should consult the organizers’ official contest instructions for categories, voting rules and deadlines.
