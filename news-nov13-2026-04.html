FIESTAJAM'S BIG AMBITION: MAKE MARBLE FALLS A SONGWRITER DESTINATION

After its November festival, organizers are looking to build a stronger year-round music community.

HIGHLAND LAKES LIVE · Original report or event listing: November 10, 2026

FiestaJAM organizers say they want Marble Falls to become an important destination for Texas songwriters. The November 6–8 festival brought established performers and emerging talent together at Round Mountain Cider Mill. The nonprofit also supports youth music education and continues regular songwriter gatherings, giving the region a way to build on the festival beyond one weekend.
