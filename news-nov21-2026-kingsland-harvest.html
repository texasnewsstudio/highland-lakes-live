EVERYONE'S INVITED! KINGSLAND PREPARES FREE SUNDAY FEAST

Sharing the Harvest plans its third annual community Thanksgiving meal.

Sharing the Harvest is scheduled to host its third annual Thanksgiving Community Meal on Sunday, November 22, from 4 to 6:30 p.m. at 2480 Rosehill Drive in Kingsland. The event is advertised as a no-cost meal open to the community.

The gathering is one of several local efforts to make the holiday more welcoming. People interested in attending should verify arrangements with organizers before arriving.
