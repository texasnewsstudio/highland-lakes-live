COST OVERRUN CLASH! MARBLE FALLS WATER PROJECT UNDER FIRE

A city council member disputes further cost overruns tied to the One Water project.

The cost of Marble Falls' One Water project is again under scrutiny after a city council member challenged further cost overruns, according to local reporting published November 19.

The disagreement raises questions about project spending, oversight and how the city explains additional costs to the public. The reporting establishes a dispute over overruns, not a finding of wrongdoing.

Highland Lakes Live will distinguish confirmed project figures and official decisions from political criticism as additional records become available.
