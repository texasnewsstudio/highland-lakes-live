Former Burnet County Judge Files Federal Civil Rights Lawsuit

Former Burnet County Judge James Oakley has filed a federal civil rights lawsuit naming Burnet County, the sheriff and a former district attorney.

The filing is a legal allegation, not a court finding. The claims and any responses from the named parties will need to be evaluated through the judicial process. Residents following the matter should distinguish allegations in the complaint from facts established by a court.

The lawsuit was reported October 27. No ruling on the merits is implied by the filing itself.
